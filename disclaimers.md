## Recording and transcription

Audio relayed through node 588416 may be recorded and transcribed as part
of an experimental AI-assisted net-logging pipeline. Recording happens at
the node; transcripts are generated locally and are not published in full.
See [The Transcription Project](transcription-project.md) for details on
how this works.

Amateur radio communications carry no expectation of privacy under FCC
Part 97 or federal wiretap law, and this project doesn't change that —
this notice is provided as a courtesy, not because it's legally required.

## Not a system of record

This node's transcription pipeline is an **asynchronous, best-effort**
service, not live captioning and not a system of record. It should not be
relied on for:

- Fire, EMS, or hospital coordination
- Emergency Operations Center (EOC) traffic
- Formal ARES/RACES traffic
- SKYWARN reporting

Known limitations include occasional missed or garbled callsigns,
particularly at the start of a transmission, and general transcription
error rates inherent to automatic speech recognition. *(Adjust this list
to whatever you're comfortable stating publicly.)*

This node and its associated projects are operated as a hobby. Any
donations solicited elsewhere for related projects (Random Wire, EtherHam)
are voluntary and go toward covering costs, not profit.
